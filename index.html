Explore the chapter’s satellite weather network and student-run internet radio station.

Satellite reception

SkyPulse

Launched in November 2024, SkyPulse combines satellite observations with a LoRaWAN weather network to study Bengaluru’s microclimates. It tracks local weather and urban heat for short-range forecasting.

Internet radio

Campus Radio Station

A student-built app connects to an Icecast server for live broadcasts, recorded programmes and campus announcements. The team chose online streaming after exploring the licensing and spectrum requirements of campus FM.
